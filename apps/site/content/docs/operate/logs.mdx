---
title: Logs
description: One view for build, deploy, runtime and activity logs, with filters, search and follow.
---

The **Logs** view of a service merges four sources in one timeline, sorted by timestamp.

| Source | What it is | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| `build` | Image build output of each deployment | Stored by the panel |
| `deploy` | Deployment steps: pull, start, healthcheck, swap | Stored by the panel |
| `runtime` | The container's stdout and stderr | Stays on the server; read on demand with `docker logs` |
| `activity` | Who changed what on the service, and when | Stored by the panel |

Runtime logs are never copied to the panel in bulk. Each page reads a bounded window from the
server, so a stopped or unreachable server only hides the `runtime` source; the other three
still load and the view says which source is unavailable.

## Filters and limits

- **Time range**: the last 15 minutes by default, at most 24 hours. A wider range is cut to its
  newest 24 hours and the page is marked as truncated.
- **Level**: `info`, `warn` or `error`. A level keeps entries at that level or above.
- **Search**: plain text, at most 200 characters, case-insensitive.
- **Page size**: 200 lines by default, at most 1000. When a range has more than one page, the
  view shows the newest lines; narrow the range or add a search to see older ones.
- A line longer than 8 KiB is cut and marked as truncated.
- **Follow** streams new lines as they arrive. Too many open streams for one service or account
  is refused with `RUNTIME_LOG_FOLLOW_LIMIT_REACHED`; close one and try again.

stdout and stderr are each in order, but Docker does not guarantee their order relative to each
other, so two lines from different streams with the same timestamp can swap.

## Secrets in logs

Every line is redacted with the service's secret values before it leaves the panel, for every
source. A secret printed by the application shows as `[REDACTED:<type>]`.

## Errors

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `LOG_QUERY_INVALID` | A source, time range, level or search term is not valid. |
| `LOG_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` | A source could not be read, usually because the server is unreachable. |
| `RUNTIME_LOG_FOLLOW_LIMIT_REACHED` | Too many live log streams are open. |
