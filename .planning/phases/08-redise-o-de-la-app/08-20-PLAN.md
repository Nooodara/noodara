<objective>
Extend the one press definition to the overlay and navigation items, and make good on the brief's hardest motion prohibition: nothing initiated from the keyboard animates.

Purpose: §9 #10 is a review-blocking rule and it is the easiest rule in the brief to regress silently. Pitfall P14 plus the pre-existing `onEscapeKeyDown` gate mean the keyboard branch must live inside each component, reading the close source the shared `useCloseSource` primitive already tracks — never at a call site and never through Radix's escape hatches. This plan runs after both wave-9 motion plans because it asserts the `Sheet`'s own Esc branch (08-12) alongside its own three, and consumes `PRESS_CLASSES` (08-13).
Output: press feedback on menu and nav items, keyboard-close suppression in `Dialog`/`RowMenu`/`AccountMenu`, and an E2E spec that measures all four overlays.
</objective>
